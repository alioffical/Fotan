Is the chat on this page a live model call?

This preview is the intake pattern, running locally in your browser so the storefront stays fast and private. The installed concierge on a client site is configured for that practice's services, hours, and calendar. Activation is a real email to the desk, not a simulated send.

Can I buy the setup without the monthly retainer?

Yes. The 48-hour turnkey setup is a one-time $997 install with no required monthly contract. The managed retainer is the ongoing option when you want text-back, a dedicated number, and script tuning kept current.
